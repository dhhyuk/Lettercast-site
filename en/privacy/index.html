Privacy Policy

Effective: November 9, 2026

Lettercast ("the app") is an iOS app for reading, listening to, and summarizing newsletters. The app creates a dedicated Lettercast email address for you (for example, `k7m2x9q4ta@lettercast.my`) and shows you the newsletters sent to it. It never asks for your name, email, or phone number, and works without signing in.

Information we process

  • Your Lettercast address and account identifier: generated randomly when you first open the app. They contain nothing that identifies you.
  • Mail sent to your Lettercast address: the full message, including sender, subject, and body. These are newsletters you subscribed to with this address or forwarded from another mailbox.
  • Device token and public key: a random token the app uses to connect to our server (the server stores only an irreversible hash) and the public key used to lock your mail.
  • Newsletters you subscribe to: when mail from a new sender arrives, the app asks "Is this a newsletter you subscribed to?" Only for senders you confirm, or newsletters you subscribe to from the Discover tab, we store the sender's name, email address, and mailing-list identifier (List-Id) with your account. No mail content is included. Senders you decline are not stored.
  • Push notification token (if notifications are on): used only to tell you about new newsletters.
  • Connection data: your IP address may be processed briefly when the app connects. An irreversible hash of it is used for one day to limit abuse.

How we use it

  • To receive, store, and deliver newsletters sent to your Lettercast address
  • To show newsletters by date and publisher, tidy them in reader mode, and read them aloud with on-device speech synthesis
  • To create summaries with the on-device model (Apple Foundation Models)
  • To notify you about new newsletters and show subscription confirmation emails
  • To prevent spam and abuse (per-address volume limits, filtering blocked senders)
  • To recommend newsletters you may like, based on the newsletters you subscribe to

We do not use any of this for advertising, tracking, or training AI models. We do not sell or share your mail. Usage statistics to improve the app are collected separately, only as described in "Usage statistics" below.

How your mail is stored

  • When a message arrives, the Lettercast mail server immediately locks (encrypts) it with a public key created on your iPhone. The key that unlocks it (the private key) exists only on your iPhone (and in your iCloud Keychain), never on our server. No one, including the developer, can read the mail stored on the server.
  • At the moment of arrival, the server briefly processes the message to lock it and to filter spam. The sender address, sender domain, and message identifier are kept only as irreversible hashes (for blocking and duplicate checks). The message size and arrival time are kept as is.
  • Stored mail is kept until you delete your account, so you can get it back after reinstalling or on a new iPhone. If you choose "Also delete from server" when deleting a message, it is removed from the server too.
  • Newsletter subscription records are deleted when you delete your account, mark that sender as not a newsletter, or unsubscribe.
  • If no device opens the app for one year, we send a notification and delete the address and all stored mail 30 days later.
  • Attachments are stored locked together with the original message but are not opened in the app. Messages larger than 5 MB are not accepted.

Information stored on your device

  • Your newsletter list, read status, archive, highlights, notes, and summaries are stored in the app's private space on your iPhone and are included in your device's iCloud Backup if you use it (so you can restore them on a new iPhone). The body cache (up to 200 MB) is not backed up.
  • Your account information and private key are stored in the iOS Keychain and, if you use iCloud Keychain, synced end-to-end encrypted to devices signed in to your Apple Account.
  • Summaries (Apple Foundation Models) and listening (iOS speech synthesis) run entirely on your iPhone. Your mail is never sent anywhere else to be summarized or read aloud.

Service providers and international transfers

| Recipient | Purpose | Data | Location | |---|---|---|---| | Cloudflare, Inc. | Receiving mail, storing locked mail and account data, communicating with the app | Mail sent to your Lettercast address (locked on arrival), account identifier and address, newsletters you subscribe to (sender name and email address), device token hash, public key, IP address (briefly) | Cloudflare's data centers worldwide, including the United States | | Apple Inc. | Delivering push notifications (if enabled) | Push token, message identifier (no subject or sender) | United States | | Google LLC (Firebase Analytics) | Usage statistics (can be turned off in Settings) | Feature usage described in "Usage statistics", a random per-install identifier, app and device information (no mail content or addresses) | Google data centers, including the United States |

  • When and how: over the network (TLS-encrypted) when mail arrives and when the app syncs.
  • Retention: as described in "How your mail is stored."
  • How to opt out: stop using the app, or delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account. You will no longer receive newsletters at your Lettercast address.

Other network connections

  • Publishers' servers: when loading images inside a newsletter and publisher icons. You can turn off remote images in Settings, and tracking pixels that report opens are blocked by default.
  • Unsubscribe: only when you confirm, a request is sent to the unsubscribe address listed in the newsletter.
  • Recommended newsletters: when you open the Discover tab, the app downloads the list and newsletter logos, identical for everyone, from the Lettercast server (api.lettercast.my, Cloudflare). No user information, subscriptions, or mail is sent, and the server does not log these requests. You subscribe yourself on the publisher's page; Lettercast never submits your address for you.

Usage statistics

To learn which features are used and where people get stuck, we collect usage statistics with Google's Firebase Analytics.

  • What is sent: screens you open and features you use (for example, starting to listen, viewing a summary, creating a highlight, opening a recommended newsletter, changing a setting), a random per-install identifier (reset when you delete the app), app version, device model, iOS version and language, and an approximate region derived from your IP address
  • What is never sent: mail subjects, bodies or summaries, publisher names and sender addresses, your Lettercast address and account identifier, search terms, or the advertising identifier (IDFA). It is not used for cross-app tracking or advertising.
  • Turning it off: Settings → Privacy → turn off "Share usage statistics" and nothing more is sent. Deleting your account also resets this device's analytics identifier.
  • Retention: kept for up to 14 months under Google Analytics data retention settings, then deleted.

The app contains no advertising or crash reporting SDKs.

How we protect your data

  • All connections use HTTPS (TLS); the app refuses unencrypted connections.
  • Mail stored on the server can be opened only with your device key, as described above. Server storage is also encrypted at rest (AES-256).
  • Data stored on your device is encrypted by iOS Data Protection.
  • Server logs never contain mail content (subject, sender, or body).
  • Reporting security issues: please email sss989870@gmail.com.

Your rights

  • Deletion: Settings → Delete Account immediately deletes your address, stored mail on the server, and data on the device. Server backups are fully erased within 30 days. Deleted addresses are never issued to anyone else.
  • Changing your address: Settings → Get a New Address. Your old address keeps accepting mail only from senders who already wrote to it, for 30 days.
  • Blocking senders: you can stop receiving mail from senders you don't want.
  • For other requests (access, correction, suspension of processing), contact us below. Because accounts have no name or email, we will ask you to confirm ownership in a way that can be verified within the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 14.
