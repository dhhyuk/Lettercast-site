Today's edition

A fresh paper every morning

See how many newsletters arrived today and how long they take to read or to listen to. Finish them all and the edition is done.

  • Finds the newsletters you subscribe to when you connect
  • Collects only the publishers you check — personal mail is left alone
  • Read, save, or hide with a swipe
Summaries

Start with the summary

Open a newsletter and see a one-line summary and key points first, with to-dos and important links pulled out.

  • Made on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence
  • Today's Briefing: every newsletter from today, summarized
  • Shows how much time the summary saved you
Listen

Your commute, like the radio

One tap reads today's newsletters aloud, in order. It keeps going with the screen off or in other apps.

  • Pause, skip 15 seconds, or jump to the next from the Lock Screen
  • 0.75× to 2× speed, and pick up where you left off
  • In reader mode, follow the sentence being read
Reader mode

Just the words

Strip away cluttered email layouts and read the text in a clean font and comfortable spacing — or switch back to the original anytime.

  • Adjust text size and line spacing
  • Highlight sentences and add notes
  • Tracking pixels that report opens are blocked by default
Publishers

Tidy up your subscriptions

See how much you get and how much you read from each publisher. Lettercast points out the ones you've stopped reading, and you can unsubscribe right in the app.

  • New subscriptions are flagged as new publishers
  • Exclude mail that isn't a newsletter
  • Supports one-click unsubscribe
Archive

The lines you loved, kept

Highlights, notes, and saved newsletters live in your Archive, so the best sentences from past issues are easy to find again.

  • Tap a highlight to jump back to where it was
  • Save newsletters you want to reread
  • Restore hidden newsletters anytime

Privacy

Your mail never leaves
this iPhone

Lettercast has no servers. Mail comes from Google straight to your iPhone, and storage, summaries, and speech all happen on device.

No servers

Not even the developer can see your mail. No ads, analytics, or trackers.

On-device AI

Summaries use Apple Intelligence and listening uses iPhone voices. Your mail is never sent to an outside AI.

Read-only access

Gmail is connected read-only. Extra access is requested only if you turn on label organizing, and mail is never deleted or sent.

Lettercast's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
