Lettercast

Read, listen to, and summarize your newsletters

Lettercast gathers the newsletters you get in Gmail so you can read them like a newspaper, listen like a radio show, and skim them as summaries — on iPhone.

Today's edition

Today's newsletters with reading and listening time at a glance. Lettercast finds your subscriptions when you connect, and only collects the ones you check.

Listen

One tap plays today's newsletters in order, even with the screen off.

Summaries

A one-line summary, key points, and Today's Briefing — made on device with Apple Intelligence.

Comfortable reading

Original or reader mode, highlights and notes, tracking-pixel blocking, and unsubscribe in the app.

Your mail never leaves your device

Lettercast has no servers. Mail comes from Google straight to your iPhone, and storage, summaries, and speech all happen on device. Not even the developer can see your mail. No ads, no tracking.

Lettercast's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Requires an iPhone with iOS 18 or later and a Gmail account. Summaries are available on iPhone models that support Apple Intelligence, with iOS 26 or later. Gmail is a trademark of Google LLC. Lettercast is not affiliated with Google.
