FAQ

I don't see my newsletters

Make sure you subscribed with your Lettercast address. If a confirmation email arrived, tap its link under "Needs Confirmation" at the top of Today to finish subscribing.

Can I move newsletters I get at another address?

Change the email in the publisher's subscription settings to your Lettercast address, and the next issue will arrive in Lettercast. Find your address in the app's Settings.

There's no summary button

Summaries need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, iOS 26 or later, and Apple Intelligence turned on. Right after turning it on, wait while the model downloads.

How do I change the voice?

Choose a voice in Settings > Listen. You can download more natural voices in iPhone Settings > Accessibility > Read & Speak > Voices.

Is my mail stored anywhere?

Mail sent to your Lettercast address is stored locked on our server. The key to open it is only on your iPhone (and iCloud Keychain), so not even the developer can read it. Delete everything anytime in Settings > Delete Account.

What if I get a new iPhone?

With iCloud Keychain on for the same Apple Account, your address and newsletters carry over. Otherwise, keep the recovery code from Settings somewhere safe beforehand.
