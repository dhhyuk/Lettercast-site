Support

I don't see my newsletters

Lettercast collects newsletters only from the publishers you confirmed. Check the "Possible Newsletters" list in the Publishers tab, and make sure the mail arrived in the last 90 days.

There's no summary button

Summaries need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, iOS 26 or later, and Apple Intelligence turned on. Right after turning it on, wait while the model downloads.

How do I change the voice?

Choose a voice in Settings > Listen. You can download more natural voices in iPhone Settings > Accessibility > Read & Speak > Voices.

How do I revoke Google access?

Tap Settings > Disconnect and Delete All Data in the app. You can also remove Lettercast in Google Account > Security > Your connections to third-party apps & services.

Is my mail stored anywhere?

No. Mail is stored only on your iPhone and is deleted when you sign out or delete the app.
