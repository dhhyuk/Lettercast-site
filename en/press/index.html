Descriptions

One line

An iPhone app that lets you listen to your Gmail newsletters like the radio on your commute — or just skim the summaries.

Short

Lettercast gathers the newsletters scattered across your inbox in one place. One tap plays today's newsletters in order, and when you're busy you can start with a one-line summary and key points. Lettercast has no servers, so your mail never leaves your iPhone.

Long

If you keep subscribing but rarely finish a newsletter, Lettercast can help. Connect Gmail and it finds the newsletters you subscribe to and organizes them by date. On your commute, one tap plays today's newsletters like a radio show; when you're short on time, start with one-line summaries and key points made on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence. It also offers a clean reader mode, highlights and notes, tracking-pixel blocking, and one-tap unsubscribe. Lettercast has no servers: mail comes from Google straight to your iPhone, and storage, summaries, and speech all happen on device. No ads, no tracking.

Key features

  • Today's newsletters: only your subscriptions, by date, with reading and listening time
  • Listen: today's newsletters in order, even with the screen off (Lock Screen controls, speed)
  • Summaries: one-line summaries, key points, and Today's Briefing — on device with Apple Intelligence
  • Reader mode: just the words, with highlights, notes, and tracking-pixel blocking
  • Privacy: no servers, mail never leaves the iPhone, no ads or tracking

The story

My subscriptions kept growing, but I finished only a few newsletters. What if I could listen to them like the radio on my commute, and see just the key points on busy days? Because email is sensitive, Lettercast was built from day one to do everything on the iPhone, with no servers.
